# RoleItem

**Level:** `subsection` · **Family:** [AccessControl](./families.md#accesscontrol) (domain `ProjectAuthentication`)

**Specification:** [RoleItem in Part 2](../spec/RoleItem.md)

Represents a role definition in the RBAC system. Each role includes a `name` for display and a `value` used in session data and permission checks.
