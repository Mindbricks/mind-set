# AgentHarnessPlugin

**Level:** `section` · **Family:** [AgentHarness](./families.md#agentharness) (domain `AgentHubConfig`)

**Specification:** [AgentHarnessPlugin in Part 2](../spec/AgentHarnessPlugin.md)
